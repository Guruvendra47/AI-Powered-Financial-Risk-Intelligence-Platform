# Business Requirements Document

## Project Name

AI-Powered Financial Risk Intelligence Platform

## Objective

Build an enterprise-scale platform that processes financial complaints, compliance documents, and risk reports to provide AI-powered risk intelligence and compliance insights.

## Business Goals

- Reduce manual document review
- Improve risk visibility
- Enable semantic search
- Generate AI-driven summaries
- Support compliance investigations

## Stakeholders

- Risk Analysts
- Compliance Teams
- Operations Teams
- Data Engineers
- Business Analysts
